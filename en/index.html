Office Panic Privacy Policy

Updated October 5, 2026

Office Panic is a mobile game. This page describes what data the app collects, why, and what happens to it. The developer is Madin Tlishev, an individual.

What we collect

Only anonymous game events, and only if you have given your consent:

  • shift and chapter number, shift length and how it ended;
  • how many visitors were served and how many left, the peak panic level;
  • which windows you built and upgraded, which abilities you used;
  • a random player code that is created on the device and linked to nothing;
  • technical details of a crash: device model, system version, game version and the location of the crash in the code.

What we never collect

  • your name, email address, phone number or any contact details;
  • the device advertising identifier — the app never requests it;
  • location, the list of installed apps, the contents of the clipboard;
  • photos, files, microphone, camera, contacts.

Why we need it

To see where the game is too hard or unclear: on which shifts people lose, which features they never use, where it crashes. The data is used only to improve the game. We do not sell it, pass it to ad networks or use it to show ads — the game has no ads.

Consent

We ask for permission once, before the first game. Declining changes nothing: the game works in full, nothing is restricted. You can change your decision at any time in the game settings: main screen, gear icon, the "Help improve the game" switch.

Who processes the data

Collection and storage are done by Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), a service of Google LLC. Its processing is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Events are stored in the project for 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect any age data. The age rating is shown on the app's store page.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent in the game settings. To request deletion of the anonymous events already collected, write to us — we will need your player code to find your data; it is shown in the pause menu and on the policy page inside the game. Step-by-step instructions and timelines are on a separate page: Data deletion request (in Russian).

Contact

Questions and requests: madin.tlishev@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
